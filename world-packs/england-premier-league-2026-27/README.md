# English Premier League 2026/27

## Included coverage

- 1 country: England
- 1 competition: Premier League 2026/27
- 20 real-world clubs
- Club identifiers, display names, home cities and simplified in-game colour pairs
- Source and redistribution provenance in `world-data-sources.json`

## Data source and rights

The club participant list and upstream club-name identifiers are based on the OpenFootball England 2026/27 Premier League fixture file. OpenFootball publishes its dataset under CC0 1.0; the exact source and licence URLs are recorded in the manifest.

The CC0 declaration applies to the contributed fixture data. It does **not** grant club trademark rights or rights to official crests, player photographs, kit artwork, sponsor marks or stadium photographs. This package therefore contains no official logos, portraits or copied kit images. The colour pairs are simplified game metadata, not a reproduction of official kit artwork.

This is a real-club data package, not an official Premier League or club licence.

## Roster coverage

No real player or staff roster is included in this first package. The game may generate players through its existing world-generation path. Do not describe those generated players as the clubs' real 2026/27 squads.

## Refresh policy

The data is a season snapshot dated 2026-10-31. Re-check the source before publishing a later-season update.
